QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS.

Is LUKO a real cryptocurrency?

Yes. LUKO is an ERC-20 token on Base. The contract is public and the emission is visible on-chain.

Can more LUKO be created?

No. All 1,000,000 LUKO were issued at genesis. The contract holds no mechanism for any further emission.

Who are LUKO's founders?

Lukas Peciukonis and Denisas Bykovas. Both hold equal founder status from LUKO's genesis.

Can the founders sell their LUKO?

Yes, the tokens can be disposed of freely. Founder status is a separate matter — final and non-transferable.

Can LUKO be acquired?

Yes. LUKO has a public market on Base. Some LUKO may also be distributed directly.

Is LUKO an investment?

LUKO is not offered as an investment. The project promises no return, no price and no future value.
