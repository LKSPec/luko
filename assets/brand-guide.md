# LUKO — mark & usage

## The mark (4B)
A geometric **L**, one stroke width (stem 52, foot 46 on a 512 grid), square terminals, with a single
16-unit chamfer on the outer bottom-right corner — a cut-stone terminal, a nod to genesis/cornerstone.

Behind it, at 30 % opacity, sits a **19**: the stem's nose turns the L into a *1*, and a drawn *9*
lands its tail on the same baseline. You read **L** first; the 19 is only visible on a second look.
Never raise the whisper above 50 % — if the 19 competes with the L, the mark is wrong.

## Colour
| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Champagne (accent) | `#C9A86A` |
| Matte black (base) | `#0A0A0A` |
| Off-white (text only) | `#EDE7DA` |

Two inks, never more. No gradients, no bevels, no gloss, no outer glow.

## Token listing
Use `logo-dark-256.png` or `logo-dark-512.svg` for wallet and DEX submissions (Base token list, CoinGecko,
Uniswap). The mark is drawn to sit at the same optical weight as ETH and USDC in a 32 px row —
do not add padding, the circle is already full-bleed.

## Clear space & minimum size
Clear space = the stem width (52 units / ~10 % of the diameter) on all sides.
Minimum: 24 px with the 19, 16 px without.

## Don'ts
Don't rotate, outline, or recolour the mark. Don't put the gold mark on mid-tone backgrounds —
black tile or off-white only. Don't set LUKO in a script or rounded typeface; the wordmark is
light-weight grotesque, letter-spaced, uppercase.
